# Human annotation protocol — PROPOSED

Create independently sampled items that isolate the target skill while varying whether the source skill is logically required. Document the freeze date, competence rubric, granularity, and definitions for needed, incidental, ambiguous and absent. Obtain redistribution permission. Assign two blinded annotators, shuffle candidate direction and match item difficulty. Include reverse-direction, negative and known-control items. Prevent annotators from seeing model outputs, method labels or downstream probe results.

Measure inter-rater agreement and show uncertain or multi-skill items. Resolve disagreements through independent adjudication with an explanation. Preserve both initial labels, all versions, anonymized IDs, rationale and SHA. Hold certain examples back for preregistered probes. This annotation helps choose interventions and interpret supported transfer, but does not by itself prove the effect of training a skill.
